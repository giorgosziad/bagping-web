How the tag works

Three steps. Then you just travel.

No setup at the airport, no map to watch. The tag does one job and does it the instant it matters.

1

Drop it in

Pair the tag once in the free app, then tuck it into your suitcase. That's the whole setup.

2

It listens for you

When your bag rolls onto your carousel, the tag comes into Bluetooth range of your phone.

3

You get pinged

Your phone buzzes: your bag is here. Step forward while the belt is still crowded.

Proximity, not GPS. BagPing uses Bluetooth to tell you when your bag is near you — reaching the belt within range. It doesn't draw a live map or track your bag across the world; it tells you the one thing that matters at baggage claim: it's here.

Privacy Policy

BagPing — a Bionectech, Inc. product · Last updated 2026

What we collect

We collect the account details you give us (email), and the pairing information needed to link your tag to your phone. We do not sell your data.

How the AI Travel Crew works

When you ask the Crew a question, we process that request to answer it — flight lookups, airport guidance, claim drafting, and policy questions you paste in. You control what you share.

What we never do

BagPing uses a Bluetooth proximity signal between your tag and your phone. We do not run a global location network and we do not track your bag across the world — the tag simply pings your own phone when it is nearby.

Your control

You can unpair a tag, delete your account, and remove your data at any time from within the app.

BagPing — a Bionectech, Inc. product · Last updated 2026

The app is free

The BagPing app and the AI Travel Crew are provided free of charge. The physical tag is a one-time purchase.

What the tag does

The tag uses Bluetooth Low Energy to notify your paired phone when it comes into proximity — for example, when your bag reaches the carousel. It is a proximity aid, not a guaranteed-recovery or global-tracking device.

The AI Travel Crew

The Crew provides informational assistance — flight tracking, airport guidance, claim drafting, and help reading your own insurance policy. It assists you; it does not act as your airline, insurer, or legal representative.
