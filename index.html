Privacy Policy

BagPing, a product of Bionectech, Inc. Last updated 2026. This policy explains what we collect, why, and the control you keep.

What we collect

We collect the minimum needed to run BagPing: your account details (phone number for verification), the bags you add, and your language preference. Bag-proximity detection happens between the BLE tag and your own phone — BagPing does not track your bag on a global location network.

How the AI Travel Crew uses your input

When you chat with a Crew specialist, your message is processed to generate a reply. Don't paste more personal detail than a question needs. Insurance text you submit to the Policy Reader is used only to answer you in that session.

What we never do

We don't sell your personal data. We don't show third-party advertising inside the app. We don't share your travel patterns with airlines or insurers.

Your control

You can delete a bag, clear your chats, or remove your account at any time. Removing your account erases your stored profile from our systems.

What BagPing is

BagPing pairs a BLE tag with your phone to ping you when your bag is within range — typically as it reaches the carousel. It is a convenience aid, not a guaranteed global tracking or recovery service. Range depends on your phone, the environment, and Bluetooth conditions.

The tag and the app

The BLE tag is a one-time purchase. The BagPing app and the AI Travel Crew are free to use and require no subscription. Tag pricing: 1 for $49.99, 2 for $89.99, 3 for $124.99, 5 for $199.99. Tag availability and ship dates will be announced at launch.

AI Travel Crew — informational only

The AI Travel Crew provides general travel assistance: flight information, airport guidance, claim drafting and policy explanation. Its output is informational and may be incomplete or out of date. Always confirm critical details (gates, deadlines, coverage) with the airline, airport or insurer directly. Drafted claims are starting points you review and submit yourself.
